      Route too narrow: bed north side

      What we ruled out
      • Miles 60-inch dresser: excluded by the owner; west usable span is about 56.9 ± 1.4 in.
      • Head west: not allowed by this brief and the west doors leave no continuous queen headboard span.
      • Head south: headboard/nightstands or the bed conflict with the closet swing; not quietly relaxed.
      • Head east, wall anchored: clearing the east glass limits the north bed edge to 20.5 in, less than the 22-in disk route target; a wider north-side aisle overlaps the operable glass.
      • Head north, wall anchored: the clear span east of the north window leaves too little east-side access; centring the bed blocks the north glass.
      • Unknown drawer travel or unknown storage volume is not treated as a verified pass.
      Measurements that settle the next version
      • East window: from the north wall to the north end of the sill (stool) and to the outer edge of the trim (estimates 80.3 and 81.3 in), and how far the sill sticks out from the wall (estimate 2 in). This decides whether the queen headboard sits flat to the wall.
      • North nightstand width (estimate 17 in, Daniel says about 18).
      • On the west wall: from the north wall to the white door's trim (estimate 58.9) and to its hinge edge (62.15 to 62.9), and how far the north window's sill sticks out.
      • Clear room width from west wall to east wall at about 30 in high.
      • Whether the white door and the closet door must open fully, and what is behind the white door.
