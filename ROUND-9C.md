# Living room and guest bedroom

Entry points: `docs/living-p8w3k/` and `docs/guest-v2n7q/`, through the five-room house index. Build with `npm run build`; run all retained and new tests sequentially with `npm test`. Private inputs resolve exclusively through `ROOM_PROJECT_DIR` or ignored `.project-dir`. No private project path belongs in public source.

The additive `tools/home-*` adapters share the merged catalog, Flatten shapes, polygon opening routes and calibrated bedroom approach solver. They do not modify retained wall-clearance or walkway geometry. `src/home-*` provides plans, the illustrative 3D renderer, comparison, favourites, hash links and fallbacks. All measurements and engine failures are statically rendered for no-JavaScript use. `tools/home-browser.mjs --screenshots` optionally writes captures to the owning project's Round 9c render directory; the default test does not capture screenshots.

Both Today layouts are valid by explicit calibration, with **walk not measured**. Unknown movable positions remain omitted. Council hand checks are never engine outputs. Submitted proposals are retained in `source_proposal`; source IDs determine the `Council pick A1` labels, not array order. Catalog dimension disagreements are explicit failures. Unmeasured organic Amoeba contours use a labelled conservative rectangle instead of an invented contour or a shape-name rejection. Confirmed owned quantities are enforced, including one Murphy and one Wells. Swivel circles are checked against one another as well as physical footprints.

Guest geometry uses the 126.5 × 143.5 shell. Seven additional combinations of alternate shell / hinge / sliding panel are evaluated, with east/south/centred wall items reanchored. Everyday entry is at 90°, closet closed; closet-open routes are separate warnings. A pocket door has no swing. The 72 × 84 gym workout floor is an assumption, separate from the published 27.16 × 48.03 fold-down footprint.

Living routes cover all three opening pairs. The front door is outside the living room. The hearth is walkable and prohibits furniture/rugs. Swivel zones use at least half the footprint diagonal. TV size/height/eye point are assumptions and TV guides remain preferences.

Search retains two compact candidates per brief family, rehydrates full published geometry and reports real subprocess elapsed time / peak RSS. It covers discrete catalog anchors, not continuous optimisation. Generic size studies are separate analytic shell/fixed-solid studies; they do not claim complete routed passes. The office-first foldaway family has no chosen product; the optional den is off. Static 45-degree chair poses and arbitrary cross-room selections are not searched. The joined-house result names one reference combination.

For a scoped full verification, set `ROOM_TEST_EVIDENCE_DIR` to a permitted evidence directory and run `npm test -- --screenshots`. The retained browser harnesses then put their reports and paired captures under that root rather than refreshing prior-round evidence. `WALKWAY_EVIDENCE_DIR` and `ACTIVITY_WALKWAY_EVIDENCE_DIR` remain supported overrides. Only one `npm test` may run at a time.

Evidence: owning project `prototype/renders/round-9c/report.json`, per-room `*-result.json`, `browser-report.json`, `test-output-final.txt`, `shown-layouts.md`, and the render index. Independent audit seals cover the unchanged source inputs and retained clearance logic. The expected-check report explicitly reconciles three stale living-room Today coordinates against room 1.1; it never overwrites the source fixtures to force agreement.
